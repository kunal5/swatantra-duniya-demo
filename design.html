Every reader number on the site is simulated: a formula makes it, and no one is counted. The numbers move the way real readership moves, quiet at night and busy at breakfast and after dinner. Real visitor counts are kept separately and never appear on the site.

Why this logo

  • It says the name.Duniya (world) is the ring. Swatantra (free, open) is the gap. The S is both a line on a globe and the first letter of Swatantra.
  • It says what the site does.The red dot is the same live dot readers see beside every fresh number and breaking story.
  • It works small.At browser-tab size it keeps only the ring and the dot, which still read clearly.
  • It keeps the Hindi name.The masthead carries स्वतंत्र दुनिया under the English name, and is ready for a Hindi edition.

How the live news works

No one writes stories yet. A program gathers the news around the clock, and every summary is checked before it goes live. A story that two outlets report should be on the site within 10 minutes of the first report.

  1. Gather60+ news feeds every 2 minutes, world news every 15, and a web search for Lucknow, UP and Delhi every 10.
  2. SortAn AI model puts each article in its section: Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, Delhi, India, World and the rest.
  3. GroupReports of the same event become one story with many sources.
  4. SummarizeTwo or three sentences in our own words, using only facts in the reports.
  5. CheckEvery number must match a source, and claims are attributed. No survivor of a sexual offence or minor is named. A summary that fails twice shows the outlet's own headline instead.
  6. PublishThe story slides onto the page without a reload, with a link to every original report.
No adsNo ad code and no promotions, so pages stay fast and clean.
Every source linkedReaders can check any fact with the outlet that reported it.
