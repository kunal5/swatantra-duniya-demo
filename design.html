Keep the look. Add the missing controls.

A better original logoReview a wordmark, an SD monogram and an open-horizon direction in the actual masthead, phone header, favicon and print stamp. The present mark is a placeholder pending that choice.
Admin-managed image advertisementsUpload approved images, choose placements and page/city/section targeting, schedule dates, preview phone and desktop layouts, and disable a campaign. Ads are labeled and never cover text or navigation.
Rights-checked news photosUse verified photographs from licensed agencies or contracted local photographers. Record permission, source and credit. If the actual event photo is unavailable, use a clearly labeled file image or a text/map card.
Measured readershipCollect actual page views, permitted anonymous browser activity and validated visible engagement. Exclude known bots, staff and tests; disclose missing/opted-out traffic. Browser counts are not proof of distinct people.

Advertisements belong in the layout

Corner ads are panels beside the newspaper name, as selected by the owner. No floating corner ads. Additional placements include a header banner, in-feed blocks, wide-screen side rails and a footer area. Narrow layouts move or hide side placements according to an admin preview.

Images are possible—with publication rights

Many publishers license photographs. For local events, investigate local contributors and Indian agencies such as PTI, ANI or IANS; for broader coverage, AP and Reuters offer editorial licensing. Coverage and price need confirmation. Credit alone is not permission, and a government press photo is not automatically free to reuse.

Keep originals and rights evidence private. Publish approved renditions with accurate captions and credits. Do not present an old photo or an AI illustration as a photograph of a current incident.

Reuters licensing · AP photo licensing · Open-licence reuse requirements

Reports with a traceable source

Actual events→Validation and exclusions→PostgreSQL records→Versioned reports and evidence

Admin reports cover articles, sections, cities and ad performance over a selected month or year. Each report records its time window, metric definitions, exclusions, cutoff and source evidence. Preserve checksummed source extracts for annual reports under the approved retention and erasure policy. A new site cannot produce a year of history before it has collected that history.

India storage, with an explicit public-cache boundary

Proposed: DigitalOcean managed website and news worker, PostgreSQL and image storage in Bangalore. Database records, original uploads, backups, report evidence, admin data and reader analytics stay stored in India. Public news pages and public image renditions may use global caches. External AI/search is allowed; private admin data and reader identifiers are not sent to those services.

Before choosing paid resources, verify backup and log storage locations, private-response cache headers, restoration, costs and security controls. Regional availability is not a compliance certificate. The proposal is not yet deployed.
